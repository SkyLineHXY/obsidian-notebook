# FPO++: Flow Policy Gradients for Robot Control (Yi 2026)

> Brent Yi, Hongsuk Choi, Himanshu Gaurav Singh, et al. Amazon FAR / UC Berkeley / Stanford / CMU.  
> **Note:** This paper builds on McAllister 2025 (source #29, original FPO). It is distinct from [[wiki/sources/vla-rl/2026-05-25 π0-FPO RFT Flow-VLA (Lyu 2025)]], which independently coined the same "FPO" name for a similar but separate algorithm.

## TL;DR

FPO++ is an improved Flow Policy Optimization algorithm for training robot control policies with online RL. Starting from McAllister's FPO (CFM loss ratio as IS ratio surrogate), two targeted fixes make it robust for challenging robotics: **(1) per-sample ratio clipping** and **(2) an asymmetric trust region (ASPO)**. Validated on quadruped locomotion, humanoid motion tracking, and bimanual manipulation; successful sim-to-real transfer onto Booster T1 and Unitree G1 humanoids.

## 核心问题

Likelihood-based policy gradient (PPO on Gaussian policies) 是机器人 RL 的主流，但约束 action 表达为简单高斯分布。Flow-matching 策略表达能力更强，但其 action log-likelihood 计算需对 ODE 积分迹进行数值求解，代价 $\mathcal{O}(d^3 T)$。McAllister 的 FPO 用 CFM loss ratio 作为 IS ratio 的代理，但在真实机器人任务（腿足运动、仿人控制）中不稳定。

## 方法：FPO++

### 基础：FPO IS Ratio 代理

FPO 用条件流匹配（CFM）损失差代替 IS ratio：

$$
\hat{\rho}_\text{FPO}(\theta) = \exp\!\Big(\hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{CFM},\theta_\text{old}}(a_t; o_t) - \hat{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{CFM},\theta}(a_t; o_t)\Big)
$$

其中 $\hat{\mathcal{L}}_\text{CFM}$ 是对 $N_\text{mc}$ 个 $(\tau_i, \epsilon_i)$ 对的 Monte Carlo 平均。

### 改进 1：Per-Sample Ratio

标准 FPO 先对所有 MC 样本平均后再计算一个 ratio（即 clip 作用于平均后的值）。FPO++ 为**每个样本 $(\tau_i, \epsilon_i)$ 独立计算 ratio**：

$$
\hat{\rho}_{\text{FPO}++}^{(i)}(\theta) = \exp\!\Big(\ell_{\theta_\text{old}}^{(i,t)} - \ell_\theta^{(i,t)}\Big)
$$

同一 advantage $\hat{A}_t$ 被所有样本共享。好处：每个 $(\tau_i, \epsilon_i)$ 对独立触发 clip，提供更细粒度的信任区域。

### 改进 2：ASPO（Asymmetric SPO）

对于**正 advantage** 动作（更好的动作，梯度减少 CFM loss）：使用标准 PPO clip。  
对于**负 advantage** 动作（较差的动作，梯度增加 CFM loss）：使用 SPO 目标（不截断而是用二次惩罚拉回）：

$$
\psi_\text{SPO}(\rho_\theta, \hat{A}_t) = \rho_\theta \hat{A}_t - \frac{|\hat{A}_t|}{2\varepsilon^\text{clip}}(\rho_\theta - 1)^2
$$

合并为 ASPO：

$$
\psi_\text{ASPO}(\rho, \hat{A}_t) = \begin{cases} \psi_\text{PPO}(\rho, \hat{A}_t), & \hat{A}_t \geq 0 \\ \psi_\text{SPO}(\rho, \hat{A}_t), & \hat{A}_t < 0 \end{cases}
$$

ASPO 防止负 advantage 动作的 CFM loss 剧烈增加，从而**保护熵**并稳定变分间隙。

### 完整 FPO++ 目标

$$
\max_\theta\; \mathbb{E}_{\pi_{\theta_\text{old}}} \!\left[\sum_{i=1}^{N_\text{mc}} \psi_\text{ASPO}\!\Big(\hat{\rho}_{\text{FPO}++}^{(i)}(\theta),\, \hat{A}_t\Big)\right]
$$

## 实验结果

| Task Domain | Setup | Key Result |
|---|---|---|
| Quadruped locomotion | Train from scratch | Improved gait quality vs Gaussian-PPO baseline |
| Humanoid motion tracking | Train from scratch | Stable long-sequence tracking |
| Manipulation | Fine-tune from demos | More robust to low-quality base policy vs DPPO |
| Sim-to-real | Booster T1 + Unitree G1 | Direct deployment; reduced sampling steps |

**Fine-tuning robustness**: DPPO fails when base policy success rate ≲ 10% (e.g., Can task); FPO++ succeeds even with weak base policies.

## 与相关方法的对比

| 方法 | IS Ratio 来源 | Backbone | 探索 |
|---|---|---|---|
| DPPO (Ren 2024) | Noise log-prob (DDPM) | Diffusion | Stochastic noise |
| ReinFlow (Zhang 2026) | Noise injection + Markov PG | Flow | Noise injection |
| FPO (McAllister 2025, #29) | CFM loss ratio (per-action avg) | Flow | CFM sampling |
| **FPO++ (Yi 2026, #43)** | **CFM loss ratio (per-sample)** | **Flow** | **Flow + ASPO** |
| Lyu-FPO (Lyu 2025, #45) | CFM loss ratio (π0 latent space) | Flow VLA | Latent Euler |
